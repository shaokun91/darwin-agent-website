Our thesis

Intelligence should not reset at the end of a context window.

Today’s agents are powerful but static. They repeat mistakes, discard hard-won experience, and depend on engineers to redesign their behavior. We are building the machinery for agents to accumulate experience, diagnose failure, and improve the systems around them.

Our unit of evolution is not only the model.

It is the entire agent system.

Research agenda

From experience
to evolution

A self-evolving agent needs more than memory. It needs a closed loop that can preserve evidence, locate the source of failure, propose changes, and verify that those changes generalize.
